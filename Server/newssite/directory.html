🌎 About India Directory

India is a vast country with 28 states and 8 union territories. Each state is divided into districts, which are further divided into sub-districts called Mandals (also known as Tehsils or Talukas in some states). Mandals contain multiple villages and towns.

This directory provides comprehensive information about each administrative division, including population data, famous places, connectivity details, and important government offices such as police stations, hospitals, fire stations, banks, and schools along with their contact information and location on Google Maps.

🏛 Administrative Structure

State → Governed by Chief Minister and Governor. Contains multiple districts.
District → Headed by District Collector/Magistrate. Contains multiple mandals/tehsils.
Mandal/Tehsil → Administered by Tahsildar. Contains multiple villages and towns.
Village → Basic administrative unit. Governed by Village Panchayat (Sarpanch).

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

How many states and union territories are there in India? ▼
India has 28 states and 8 union territories as of 2024. The newest additions were the reorganization of Jammu & Kashmir into two union territories (J&K and Ladakh) in 2019.
What is a Mandal/Tehsil? ▼
A Mandal (also called Tehsil, Taluka, or Block in different states) is a sub-district administrative division. It is the level between a district and a village. Each mandal is headed by a Tahsildar or Mandal Revenue Officer (MRO) who handles land records, revenue collection, and local governance.
How can I find government offices near me? ▼
Navigate to your state, then district, then mandal in this directory. Each level shows nearby government offices including police stations, hospitals, fire stations, banks, and schools with their contact details and a "Get Directions" button that opens Google Maps for navigation.
What is the difference between a Village, Town, and City? ▼
In India, a settlement with population less than 5,000 is typically classified as a village. Towns have populations between 5,000 and 1,00,000 with urban characteristics. Cities have populations above 1,00,000. Metropolitan cities have populations above 10,00,000.
How accurate is the data in this directory? ▼
The directory data is compiled from Census of India, government records, and public databases. Population figures are based on Census 2011 and estimated projections. Contact details for government offices are sourced from official government portals. We recommend verifying critical information with local authorities.
