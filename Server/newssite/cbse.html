💡 Preparation Tips for Class 10 Board Exams 2026

1. Master NCERT Textbooks First
70-80% of board exam questions come directly from NCERT. Read every line, including boxed items and activities. Complete all in-text and exercise questions.
2. Focus on High-Weightage Chapters
Science: Life Processes (6 marks), Electricity (7 marks), Light (6 marks). Maths: Triangles (8 marks), Trigonometry (12 marks), Statistics & Probability (11 marks).
3. Practice Diagrams and Numericals
Biology diagrams (heart, nephron, brain) and Physics numericals (electricity, optics) are scoring. Practice drawing neat, labeled diagrams daily.
4. Solve Previous Year Papers
Solve last 5 years' board papers with time management (3 hours). Analyze marking scheme to understand answer writing format.
5. Create Formula Sheets
Make subject-wise formula sheets for Maths, Physics, and Chemistry. Revise them daily in the last month before exams.

💡 Preparation Tips for Class 12 Board Exams & Competitive Exams

1. High-Weightage Chapters for JEE/NEET
Physics: Optics (14 marks), Electrostatics (10 marks). Chemistry: Electrochemistry (9 marks), Aldehydes & Ketones (8 marks). Focus on these for both boards and JEE/NEET.
2. Practice Numerical Problems Daily
Physics and Chemistry require extensive numerical practice. Solve HC Verma, NCERT Exemplar, and previous year JEE/NEET questions.
3. Understand Derivations and Mechanisms
Don't memorize derivations. Understand the logic. For Chemistry, learn reaction mechanisms step-by-step.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about CBSE study materials and board exams

What is CBSE and which schools follow it? +
CBSE (Central Board of Secondary Education) is a national-level board of education in India for public and private schools, controlled and managed by the Union Government. Over 27,000 schools in India and 240 schools in 25 foreign countries are affiliated to CBSE. CBSE follows the NCERT curriculum and conducts board examinations for Class 10 and Class 12.
Which chapters have highest weightage in Class 10 Science? +
In CBSE Class 10 Science 2025-26, the highest weightage chapters are: Life Processes (6 marks), Electricity (7 marks), Light - Reflection and Refraction (6 marks), Carbon and Its Compounds (5 marks), and How Do Organisms Reproduce (5 marks). Chemistry and Biology units carry 25 marks each, making them crucial for scoring.
What is the Class 10 exam pattern for 2026? +
CBSE Class 10 board exams 2026 consist of 80 marks theory + 20 marks internal assessment. The question paper includes: 20 MCQs (20 marks), 6 very short answer questions (12 marks), 7 short answer questions (21 marks), 3 case-based questions (12 marks), and 3 long answer questions (15 marks). Total exam duration is 3 hours.
How to prepare for CBSE board exams effectively? +
Start with NCERT textbooks and complete them thoroughly. Focus on high-weightage chapters first. Make short notes and formula sheets for quick revision. Solve previous year papers (last 5 years) with time management. Practice CBSE sample papers regularly. For Science, practice diagrams daily. For Maths, solve numerical problems from each chapter. Use our chapter-wise marking schemes to understand question patterns and answer writing format.
What are the best books for Class 12 Science preparation? +
For CBSE Class 12 Science, NCERT textbooks are the most important. Additionally: Physics - HC Verma (Concepts), NCERT Exemplar; Chemistry - NCERT Exemplar, OP Tandon (Organic); Mathematics - RD Sharma, NCERT Exemplar; Biology - NCERT is sufficient, Trueman's for additional practice. For JEE/NEET preparation, add coaching material and previous year question papers.
What is the difference between Maths Standard and Basic in Class 10? +
CBSE Class 10 offers two levels of Mathematics: Standard (for students planning to take Maths in Class 11) and Basic (for others). Standard Maths has more difficult questions and is required for Science stream in Class 11-12. Basic Maths has easier questions but doesn't allow you to take Maths in higher classes. Both have the same syllabus but different difficulty levels.
