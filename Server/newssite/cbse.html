❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about CBSE board, exams, and study materials

What is CBSE and which schools follow it? ▼
CBSE (Central Board of Secondary Education) is a national-level board of education in India for public and private schools, controlled and managed by the Union Government. Over 27,000 schools in India and 240 schools in 25 foreign countries are affiliated to CBSE. CBSE follows the NCERT curriculum and conducts board examinations for Class 10 and Class 12.
What is the CBSE syllabus for 2025-26? ▼
The CBSE syllabus for 2025-26 has been designed as per NEP 2020 guidelines. For Classes 9-10, it includes core subjects like English, Hindi, Mathematics, Science, and Social Science. For Classes 11-12, students can choose from Science (PCM/PCB), Commerce, or Humanities streams. The syllabus emphasizes competency-based learning with internal assessments and practical components.
How can I download NCERT solutions for free? ▼
You can access NCERT solutions for all classes (1-12) and subjects for free on this page. We provide chapter-wise solutions with detailed explanations, diagrams, and step-by-step answers. Simply select your class and subject to view or download the solutions in PDF format.
What are the best books for CBSE board exam preparation? ▼
For CBSE board exams, NCERT textbooks are the most important resource as 70-80% of questions come directly from them. Additionally, you can use: RD Sharma/RS Aggarwal for Mathematics, HC Verma for Physics, Lakhmir Singh for Science (Class 9-10), and Oswaal/Arihant sample papers for practice.
How is the CBSE Class 10 board exam pattern for 2026? ▼
CBSE Class 10 board exams 2026 are of 80 marks theory + 20 marks internal assessment. The question paper includes MCQs, short answer, and long answer questions. Mathematics has two levels: Standard (for students planning to take Maths in Class 11) and Basic (for others). Each exam is 3 hours duration.
What is the difference between CBSE and ICSE? ▼
CBSE follows NCERT curriculum and is recognized by the Government of India. It focuses on Science and Mathematics with a more structured approach. ICSE (Indian Certificate of Secondary Education) follows CISCE curriculum with a broader subject range and emphasis on English and analytical skills. CBSE is preferred for competitive exams like JEE/NEET, while ICSE provides a more comprehensive education.
How to prepare for CBSE board exams effectively? ▼
Start with NCERT textbooks and complete them thoroughly. Make short notes and formula sheets. Solve previous year papers (last 5 years). Practice CBSE sample papers with time management. Focus on understanding concepts rather than rote learning. Use our revision notes and important questions sections for targeted preparation.
